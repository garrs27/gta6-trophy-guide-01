FIELD NOTES / FAQ

Before you go.

How long is the platinum?

Plan for roughly 200 hours: 50–60 for the story, about 100 for the 100% checklist, and around 10 for cleanup.

How difficult is it?

The launch-day guide estimate is 4/10.

What can I miss?

Positive Mental Attitude. Complete Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation and all meditation spots before progressing beyond the chapter.

Can I replay missions?

No. The fact sheet lists no mission replay and no New Game+. Keep manual saves; the game provides 15 slots.

Do cheats affect trophies?

Yes. Cheats disable trophies, so keep them off during this run.

What does unconfirmed mean?

The description is public, but no guide has confirmed the exact trigger. Any suggested route is explicitly labelled as a lead.

Where is my progress saved?

Earned trophies and checklist marks stay in this browser’s local storage. Export a JSON backup before switching devices.

Why are some names hidden?

Hidden trophies can spoil story or surprise moments. The name “T**s Ass Drug Money” remains masked; its full wording is unconfirmed.
